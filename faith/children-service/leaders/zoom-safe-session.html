Zoom Safe-Session Checklist

Bible Explorers · for class leaders
Draft — to be checked by your safeguarding lead

Two vetted adults in every class. Never one-to-one with a child. A parent or carer is nearby for every child. No recording unless the safeguarding lead has approved it in writing.

Before the day

  • You and your co-host are the two adults. Both have the DBS check (or local equivalent) and safeguarding training recorded.
  • The host uses an adult's Zoom account. Children never need their own account and join as guests through a parent's device.
  • Free Zoom stops a meeting at 40 minutes. Trailblazers' class is about 42, so use a paid or non-profit account.
  • Send the link and passcode only through the class website or straight to enrolled parents. Never post it publicly or on social media.
  • Check the register: only enrolled children are expected.
  • Practise the story, props and activities once so the class runs smoothly.

Zoom settings to check

  • Passcode on.
  • Waiting room on. Admit each child one by one.
  • "Join before host" off.
  • Screen sharing: host only.
  • Chat: off (or host-only). No private chat at all.
  • Participants renaming themselves off.
  • Mute on entry on. The host unmutes on cue.
  • Auto-record off.
  • Co-host assigned. Breakout rooms not used for 5-11s.

At the start

  • Arrive first. Start with the waiting room open.
  • Admit only names you recognise. First names only on screen.
  • Check a grown-up is nearby for each child.
  • Once everyone is in, Lock Meeting.
  • Cameras are optional. Say so, and mean it.
  • Say it out loud: "No recording, no private messages, and if anything feels wrong, tell your grown-up."

During class

  • The co-host watches faces, hands and the waiting room while the storyteller tells the story.
  • Children speak only when invited. Use reactions and call-and-response instead of open mics.
  • Never ask a child to share their address, school, or details of home life.
  • No screenshots or photos of children by anyone, including leaders.
  • If someone unexpected appears: use Security → Suspend Participant Activities, remove them, and tell the safeguarding lead.

At the end

  • Say goodbye to the children, then End Meeting for All. Never leave a child in a room alone.
  • Leaders stay on until every child has left.
  • Note who attended and anything unusual, however small.
  • Pass any concern to the safeguarding lead the same day.

Ask parents to

  • Join from a shared room, not a bedroom.
  • Camera pointed at the child's face, with a plain background.
  • A grown-up stays nearby and can hear the class.
  • Anything a child says that worries you goes to the safeguarding lead.

If a child tells you something worrying

  1. Listen calmly. Do not question them or investigate. Do not promise to keep it secret.
  2. Tell them: "Thank you for telling me. I need to tell someone whose job is to help."
  3. Write down their exact words, the time and the date. Do not add your own opinion.
  4. Tell the safeguarding lead the same day. If you cannot reach them, use the backup below.
  5. A child in immediate danger: call the emergency services (999 in the UK, 112 in Ghana; check your local number).
