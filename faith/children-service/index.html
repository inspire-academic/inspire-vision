How a week works

One live class a week, with a little to do before and after. Missed a class? Watch the recap. It counts exactly the same.

1

Before class

Read the clues and work out the mystery character. It takes about five minutes on a phone or tablet.

2

Live on Zoom

Story, games, a memory verse and prayer, with two friendly leaders. A grown-up joins you.

3

After class

Take on a small mission, talk about a question with your family, and add a new place to your map.

Two crews, one story

Everyone hears the same Bible story each week, but each crew gets its own pace and its own activities.

    For parents and carers

    Children's safety comes first. This is how we are building Bible Explorers.

    Grown-up accounts only

    Children never get their own login or email. You sign in, and you choose who plays.

    First names and avatars

    No surnames, photos, ages, schools or locations. Ever.

    No chat between children

    No messages, comments or uploads. Every reply is picked from a list.

    Two vetted adults in every class

    Leaders have a DBS check (or the local equivalent) and safeguarding training, and each church has a named safeguarding lead.

    A locked-down Zoom room

    Waiting room and passcode, a class link that only appears shortly before class, chat and screen-sharing off, and no recording by default.

    Your family's information stays yours

    You can see, download and delete it. No advertising and no tracking on children's pages.

    Bible Explorers is still being built. These are the rules we are building to, and we will publish our full safeguarding and privacy policy before the first class runs. A grown-up nearby, and a device that can run Zoom, is all a child needs to join.
