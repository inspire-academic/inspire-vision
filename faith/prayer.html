A Simple Way to Pray

“Adoration. Confession. Thanksgiving. Supplication.”

A pattern as old as the Church itself — A.C.T.S.

Start by telling God who He is. Be honest about where you've fallen short. Say thank you for something real. Then ask — for yourself, for others, for what actually weighs on you today. In that order, or not — the structure is a doorway, not a rule.
