# Inspire Vision — Our Story Final Stills

This folder contains the final-cut still-image set prepared for the next Remotion / Claude Code build of the Inspire Vision “Our Story” film.

## Notes
- Use the supplied **inspire_shield_reference.jpg** as the shield reference.
- Do **not** modify or redraw the shield identity.
- Human casting target for the video should trend toward roughly **50% Black people** and the remaining **50% white and mixed-race people** across the full film.
- The gold light should be treated as a **subtle moving thread**, not a loud fixed swoosh in every frame.
