Outreach Target List — working draft, 22 Aug 2026

Who we call first

Real, publicly-verifiable UK organisations, sorted by how much of the £10k/month ladder one relationship can carry. Not a finished contact database — a starting map to work from.

How to use this: Tier 1 is where the umbrella licence deals live — one conversation can be worth £2,000+/month. Tier 2 is national-scale, some as direct customers, some as distribution partners who reach hundreds of smaller groups at once. Tier 3 is the training-course track — faster, smaller, higher-volume, and grows organically out of every Tier 1/2 conversation rather than being cold-listed in advance. Every contact route below is publicly published; none are guessed or invented — verify current details before reaching out, since staff and inboxes change.

Tier 3Individual churches & circuits

The training-course track. There are thousands of these — not worth enumerating cold. This tier fills itself in as a byproduct of every Tier 1/2 conversation (a diocese or national body can point straight at its own affiliated parishes/circuits), so the working list starts empty on purpose and grows from real referrals, not a cold list.
