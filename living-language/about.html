Krobo First

Preserving the Voice, Teaching the Language, Carrying the Culture Forward

A language is more than a collection of words. It carries memory, humour, prayer, family relationships, stories, names, songs and the voices through which one generation recognises another.

Why Krobo, and why now?

Krobo still has fluent elders, parents, teachers, traditional leaders, clergy, cultural practitioners and scholars whose living knowledge can form the foundation of this work. Language loss rarely happens suddenly — a language can remain widely known while gradually losing its place in family life, education and digital spaces. Our aim is not to portray Krobo as already lost. It is to strengthen intergenerational transmission while there remains a strong living community capable of carrying it forward.

We build with existing Ga-Dangme scholarship rather than inventing a version of Krobo of our own. The verified corpus — reviewed by fluent speakers, community representatives and language specialists — remains the single source of linguistic truth for everything this platform teaches.

First principle

The Hierarchy of Authority

Technology does not become the authority on Krobo. Software developers do not decide which variant is legitimate.

1. CommunityThe language belongs to its people.
2. Elders & SpeakersFluent voices carry the living knowledge.
3. Linguistic ExpertiseAcademic and community language authorities.
4. TechnologyA servant of transmission, never the source of truth.
Cultural Covenant

What we've committed to

  1. Elders are people, not datasets.
  2. Language variation will be preserved rather than flattened.
  3. Cultural material may carry different levels of access.
  4. Preservation does not imply unrestricted technology use.
  5. Recording a voice does not imply permission to synthetically reproduce that voice.
  6. Children require special protection.
  7. Commercial success must strengthen preservation rather than privatise it.
  8. The community should remain recognisable in what the technology produces.
