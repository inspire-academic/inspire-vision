How It Works

From village stories to future generations — here’s how we turn voices into learning, together.

1

Record elders

We work with families and communities to record the voices and stories of Krobo elders.

Verify the language

Our language team reviews and verifies each recording for accuracy and authenticity.

3

Build joyful lessons

We turn verified content into engaging audio lessons, stories and learning tools for all ages.

4

Pass it on

Children, families and schools use these resources to learn, share and keep Krobo alive.
