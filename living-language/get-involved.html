Partner as a Traditional Leader

Guide the project as a custodian, adviser or patron from the earliest stage.

Traditional leadership proposition

“We want to preserve the voices of our Krobo and Dangme elders so that their uniquely rich voices can continue teaching our children and posterity long after they are gone. In doing so, we carefully preserve our language, our culture and our heritage for generations to come.”
