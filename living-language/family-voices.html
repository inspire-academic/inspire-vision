A Family Archive Becomes a Classroom

A story a grandparent tells once can become something a grandchild returns to for years — heard, understood, practiced and explained.

1. Original audioThe recording itself, kept exactly as spoken.
2. Transcription & translationKrobo text and English meaning, once reviewed.
3. Vocabulary & pronunciationSentence-by-sentence playback and practice.
4. Comprehension & cultureAge-appropriate activities and cultural context.
